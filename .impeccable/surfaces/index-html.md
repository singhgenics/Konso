# Surface: Konso home (index.html)

Scope: the single public page of the pre-launch site, rebuilt from scratch 2026-11-19. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: South Goa holidaymakers and locals on phones; friends following the build.
Action: open the revolving menu (singhgenics.github.io/Garden/menu/table/) and know where. No bookings, no contacts, no prices, no in-page menu, no Calcutta story, no restaurant renders (the room is not built yet).
Owner brief: short and sweet, not text heavy; the food does the talking (experience, food, vibe, music); 5 foods and 4 drinks only; brand green ground, gold foil, Prata + Montserrat; smooth and lag free. Inspiration: Konso/Archive (green dish posters, flying-food clips, dark green restaurant pages).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A deep bottle-green room where the food is the only light: each screen is one lit dish or drink, poster-style, its name in gold foil, the dish crossing the name. Refuses the category default of a photo hero, "Book a table", menu cards and prices.
OWN-WORLD: bottle-green ground with an emerald light pool and the brand wallpaper's paper grain (#04120A edge to #103E20 pool); gold foil lettering (raster foil from the kit's gold ramp) in Prata; Montserrat body; uppercase tracked Montserrat labels (the kit's lockup style) on nav only; one gold solid (the Menu tab); food and drinks in saturated colour as cut-outs or feathered photographs; Veo flying-food loops.
STORY: The visitor sees dim sum, wok fire, bao, wings and four glasses, understands it is a Pan Asian kitchen and bar opening soon in Cavelossim, can turn on a lounge track, and taps through to the revolving menu.
FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark top-left, Food/Bar/Find us centre, Sound and the gold Menu tab top-right; the dumpling-on-chopsticks clip centred, chilli crisp falling into a celadon bowl; "Pan Asian kitchen" above and "and bar" below in gold foil; location line left, hours right; a few ingredients drifting at the edges.
FORM: Dark room (tasteskill direction, seed 1352695906), code-led. Brief-pinned world: the owner pinned palette, background, inspiration and later the dish motion (poster drop, chosen over a runway walk she rejected), so no concept roll was run. Signature: the poster drop (dish falls in, settles, ingredients burst behind its gold name; Steamer and Bao hand over to their live clip). seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
